FUEL & RECOVERY

🍛 Food coach

No calorie counting. The app focuses on regular meals, adequate food for growth/training, carbohydrates for activity, protein-containing foods, fruit/vegetables and fluids.

🌅 Breakfast

Choose a normal home breakfast such as poha/upma + curd, eggs + roti, oats + milk + fruit, or another balanced option.

🏫 School

Carry practical food: fruit, milk/curd, nuts, homemade sandwich, or another family-approved snack.

🏏 Before cricket

If hungry before training, choose an easy-to-digest carbohydrate-rich snack such as banana, fruit, sandwich or poha plus water.

🥛 After training

Have a normal meal/snack containing carbohydrate + a protein source + fluids.

🍽️ Dinner

Eat your normal family meal. The app is not intended to restrict food.
